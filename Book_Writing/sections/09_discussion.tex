\section{Discussion, Limitations, and Ethics}
\label{sec:discussion}

\subsection{Scientific Limitations}
The Kaggle Disease Prediction dataset is a rule-derived benchmark rather than a prospective clinical patient cohort. The absence of patient demographics (age, sex, vitals, medical history) represents an inherent constraint. Consequently, this system is strictly evaluated as an algorithmic decision-support prototype.

\subsection{Ethical Framework and Clinical Safety}
The decision-support interface is engineered with explicit safety constraints: it outputs candidate probabilities, supporting/opposing evidence, and a mandatory disclaimer advising formal clinical consultation.
